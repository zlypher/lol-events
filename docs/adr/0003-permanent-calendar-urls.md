# Permanent Calendar URLs and numeric ID keys for Team Calendars

Once published, Calendar URLs are never deleted so client subscriptions never break with 404 errors. When a League becomes inactive, its existing Calendar file is left intact on disk with its historical matches; when a Team becomes inactive (no matches within the rolling 30-day window or upcoming), its Calendar is republished empty (`0` events) rather than removed. Team Calendars are published at `cal/team/<teamId>.ical` using PandaScore's immutable numeric Team ID rather than the team slug, preventing broken URLs or split history when teams rebrand or change organizations. Human-readable team names and search are provided by the web catalog interface.
